## Deviations from Plan

### Orchestrator amendments applied

1. **Turkish "siz"** in all new TR strings (plan said "sen"). Test asserts no informal markers.
2. **No browser-language auto-switch on /eltern.** `resolveClientLocale()` and `detectsBrowserLocale()` in `locale.ts`; `LocaleProvider.getClientLocale` now calls it. On /eltern only `?lang=` or a stored switcher choice change the language, otherwise German. `/app` and all other pages are unchanged. Known limit: the provider sits in the root layout and does not re-render on client-side navigation, so the language is re-evaluated on load or on the next locale event, not on every route change.
3. No em or en dashes, brand untranslated, first person singular. Checked by test and grep.
