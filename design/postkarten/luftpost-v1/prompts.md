Verwendet: eingebautes imagegen-Tool, zwei separate Aufrufe. Nicht das CLI/API-Fallback. Die gewünschten Pixelmaße im Prompt wurden vom Tool nicht übernommen; tatsächliche Maße stehen im README. Anschließend wurden SVG-Textobjekte und ein dekorativer SVG-Markenrahmen ergänzt.

## Coverillustration

Use case: illustration-story. Create a FLAT PRINT-READY LANDSCAPE postcard illustration background, for a personal thank-you airmail postcard for the German civic project Brief-nach-Berlin. Desired output 3240 x 2280 pixels, exactly 162:114 aspect ratio (1.421:1), maximum available resolution. This is the artwork itself filling the entire image; no photograph, no mockup, no shadows outside the card. Warm almost-white ivory paper (#FAF8F5), exquisite subtle cold-pressed watercolor paper texture, bright and clean, not brown or heavily aged. An uninterrupted classic diagonal red-white-navy blue airmail stripe border all around all four edges, approximately 5 mm out of a total width of 162 mm, with lightly handpainted imperfect texture but balanced corners. Muted brick red and deep navy. The central 70% of this card should be spacious nearly blank light ivory paper to receive typography later: NO TEXT anywhere. In the bottom fifth of the card, inside the border, a delicate small Berlin skyline: recognizable Reichstag building with glass dome slightly left of center, slender Fernsehturm to the right, modest urban roofs, a few leafy trees, subtly planted rooftops; very restrained hopeful solarpunk. Pale sage green, watery slate blue, honey highlights, very gentle morning light, warm nostalgic travel-storybook watercolor, lovingly drawn architectural details and organic brushwork. Skyline is faint but identifiable, its upper edge fading softly into warm white paper, horizon and whole scene kept low (do not fill the middle of the postcard). Top-right interior stays blank, to receive a decorative stamp later. No stamp, no cancellation, no envelope on this background; those will be added as separate editable layers. No people, no rabbits, no flags, no emblems, no official symbols, no logos, no writing, no illegible pseudo-text. An original illustration, whimsical travel-book warmth and hand-painted animation-background sensibility.

## Markenillustration

Referenz: `web/public/images/email-followup-envelope.png`, nur Motiv- und Stilreferenz, kein zu veränderndes Original.

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: independent custom postage stamp motif artwork for the German project Brief-nach-Berlin. Image 1 is ONLY a motif/style reference: the tilted warm-cream red-and-blue airmail envelope flying above the glass Reichstag dome. Re-create those two recognizable motifs as a NEW high-resolution original watercolor illustration, not an enlargement of the 280px source. Desired output landscape 1800 x 1080 pixels, 5:3 ratio, maximum resolution. A single charming cream airmail envelope, front/back flap clearly recognizable, angled gently clockwise, with classic red-white-navy stripes on its edges, hovering in the upper-middle. Below it a clearly recognizable simplified glass dome of the Berlin Reichstag, architecturally plausible curved glazing with delicate slate blue lines, a soft warm honey glow. A graceful light curved flight trail connects dome and envelope. Dome should be large enough to recognize at tiny postage-stamp size; no full building and no skyline. A restrained few tiny sage watercolor leafy touches beside the base of the dome, nothing busy. Warm ivory (#FAF8F5) backdrop with gentle watercolor paper texture matching a bright nostalgic storybook airmail postcard. Subject central, full envelope and dome uncropped, generous 15% clear margins at sides and top. Leave the lowest 20% of canvas almost blank warm ivory for a separately added editable label. No writing anywhere, no letters, no logo, no denomination, no Matrix/QR code, no postage frame or perforations, no postal cancellation, no governmental emblem, no flags, no official insignia. Especially the little stamp on the envelope must be just a tiny neutral leaf picture without text or official markings. Refined airy original handpainted storybook watercolor, inviting and hopeful, elegant organic shapes, delicate but legible at small size.
