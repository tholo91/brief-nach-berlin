# 001 — Elternteil und Kind schreiben am Küchentisch

- **Type:** Image
- **Status:** approved
- **Iteration:** 1
- **Target tool:** Google Nano Banana Pro (or Midjourney v6 / Flux)
- **Aspect ratio:** 21:9 (short banner)
- **Purpose:** Banner above the headline on `/eltern`. Short on purpose, so the start button stays close to the top on phones. Shows parents that writing about school is something they do at home, together with their kid, not a big political act.

## Composition notes

- The teenager takes part, not just watches: pointing or adding a line.
- Reject variants where the parent looks angry or the scene looks like homework stress. The point is calm, shared concern.
- If the banner crops the people too tightly, ask for "people smaller, more room on the right".
