# 001 — Jugendliche schreiben auf der Treppe im Kiez

- **Type:** Image
- **Status:** approved
- **Iteration:** 1
- **Target tool:** Google Nano Banana Pro (or Midjourney v6 / Flux)
- **Aspect ratio:** 21:9 (short banner)
- **Purpose:** Banner above the headline on `/jung`. Short on purpose, so the form stays close to the top on phones. Speaks to 13 to 16 year olds: their own street, their own words.

## Prompt

```
A very wide 21:9 painted banner illustration in Studio Ghibli background
art style (Kazuo Oga): hand-painted, visible brush strokes, watercolor for
sky and distance, gouache for the middle ground. Warm, hopeful, never cute
or kitschy.

SCENE: Early evening on a quiet street corner in a green Berlin
neighborhood. Three teenagers aged about 14 to 16 sit on the stone steps
in front of an old Altbau house entrance. One writes a letter by hand on a
notebook on her knees, one reads over her shoulder, the third holds a
finished envelope with a thin red-and-blue airmail border and is already
standing up. A few meters to the right, an old-fashioned post box in muted
airmail red stands under a young linden tree. A bicycle leans against the
railing, a schoolbag lies open on the steps.

THE TEENAGERS: Clearly different, painted small and friendly: a girl with
headphones around her neck, a boy in a hoodie, a girl with a headscarf.
Real teenagers, a bit casual, not posed, not childish. Nobody looks at a
phone.

THE STREET: Altbau facades with balconies full of plants, ivy on a wall,
young linden trees, cobblestones, one tram line far in the background, a
thin row of solar panels on a distant roof. No wind turbines, nothing
futuristic.

LIGHT AND COLOR: Dominant staggered greens (forest green #2D6A4F, sage,
olive, moss). Cream #FAF8F5 and sandstone for facades and paper.
Honey-golden evening light, not orange. Blue-green shadows, never black.
Accents only: airmail red (#C1121F) on the post box, airmail blue
(#1D3557) on one jacket.

COMPOSITION: Very wide and calm. The teenagers small in the left third,
the post box in the right third, open street and sky in between. All
important elements stay inside the central horizontal band, nothing
important near the top or bottom edge, because phones crop the banner.

EXCLUSIONS: No text, no readable writing on letters, street signs or
shops, no UI. No phones, no laptops. No party colors or party logos. No
graffiti with letters. No stock-photo look, no AI glow, no glossy 3D.
```

## Composition notes

- On a 375px phone the banner is about 160px high. Check the image at that size before approving.
- If the teenagers look like small children, ask for "older, lanky, 15 years old".
