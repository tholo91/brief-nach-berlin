# 001 — Eine Klasse schreibt Briefe von Hand

- **Type:** Image
- **Status:** approved

- **Purpose:** Hero image for `/schule`, directly below the intro text. Shows teachers what the lesson looks like: a normal class, concentrated, writing real letters by hand.

## Prompt

```
A wide 16:9 painted illustration in Studio Ghibli background art style
(Kazuo Oga): hand-painted, visible brush strokes, watercolor for sky and
distance, gouache for the middle ground. Warm, hopeful, never cute or kitschy.

LIGHT AND COLOR: Dominant staggered greens (forest green #2D6A4F, sage,

EXCLUSIONS: No text, no readable writing on letters, chalkboard or
```

## Composition notes

- The wheelchair user sits in the middle of the room, not at the edge.
- Reject variants where the class looks like an exam (rows, tension). The point is focus with a bit of joy.
- If the teacher dominates, ask for "teacher smaller, in the middle ground".
