# 002 — Der Sammelumschlag einer Klasse kommt im Büro an

- **Type:** Image
- **Status:** approved
- **Iteration:** 1
- **Target tool:** Google Nano Banana Pro (or Midjourney v6 / Flux)
- **Aspect ratio:** 4:3
- **Purpose:** Image for the section "Warum Handschrift" on `/schule`. Shows the payoff for teachers and pupils: the class letters arrive as paper on a real desk and get read.

## Prompt

```
A 4:3 painted illustration in Studio Ghibli background art style
(Kazuo Oga): hand-painted, visible brush strokes, watercolor for sky and
distance, gouache for the middle ground. Warm, hopeful, never cute or kitschy.

SCENE: A calm office of a member of parliament in Berlin in the morning.
On a wooden desk lies a large brown envelope that has just been opened.
Out of it spill about twenty handwritten letters on cream paper, slightly
different sizes, some folded, a few with a small doodle in the corner.
A fictional office worker, a young woman with reading glasses and rolled
up sleeves, sits on the edge of the desk and reads one of the letters with
a surprised, warm smile. A second letter is pinned to a cork board next to
the window. A cup of tea, a fountain pen, a small plant on the desk.

THE WINDOW: Tall window with the glass dome of the Reichstag in the
distance, young linden trees and ivy in honey-gold light. Only a whisper
of green Berlin, nothing futuristic.

LIGHT AND COLOR: Dominant staggered greens (forest green #2D6A4F, sage,
olive, moss) in plants and trees. Cream #FAF8F5 and sandstone for paper
and walls. Honey-golden light, not orange. Blue-green shadows, never
black. Muted accents: the envelopes and stamps in airmail red (#C1121F)
and airmail blue (#1D3557).

COMPOSITION: Close middle shot, the pile of letters in the lower center
as the hero of the image, the reading person slightly left, window on the
right. Faces small and soft.

EXCLUSIONS: No text, no readable writing on letters, envelopes, or the
cork board, no UI. No recognizable politicians, no name plates. No party
colors or party logos. No laptops, phones or monitors on the desk. No
stock-photo look, no AI glow, no glossy 3D.
```

## Composition notes

- The letters must look handwritten by teenagers (different handwriting shapes, not printed text blocks), but never readable.
- Reject variants with a computer on the desk.
