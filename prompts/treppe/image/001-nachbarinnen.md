# Zwei Nachbarinnen auf der Haustreppe

Bild für die Treppe der Selbstwirksamkeit, Querformat 3:2. Stilreferenz ist das Kiezmotiv auf `/jung`.

## Gestaltungsprompt für Google Nano Banana

Draw a simple, hand-inked 1990s European picture-book illustration with the warmth and everyday charm of “Briefe von Felix.” Use the uploaded Kiez illustration as your guide for how little detail to draw. Landscape, 3:2.

Two neighbours sit shoulder to shoulder on the front steps of a Berlin apartment building. The younger woman holds up a letter. The older woman leans closer to read it, her shopping bag resting beside her. A raised eyebrow and a small smile tell the whole story.

View them from the front, slightly to one side. The handwritten side of the letter faces the women. We see only the completely blank cream-coloured back of the sheet. No marks or writing show through.

On the step beside them lies a separate, clearly visible airmail envelope, flap facing up. Give it a classic blue, white and red airmail border: alternating diagonal blue and red stripes with white gaps, running around all four edges. Keep the stripes on the envelope only.

Use slightly wobbly brown ink outlines, rounded cartoon faces, dot eyes, tiny noses and broad patches of flat watercolour. Suggest the setting with just three stone steps, a sage-green door and a sprig of ivy. Let large areas of cream paper breathe. Keep both faces, the letter and the striped envelope close together so the scene reads at phone size.

Aim for a drawing you could recognise from across the room. No realistic anatomy, intricate masonry, individual hair strands, cinematic lighting, glossy shading or text.
